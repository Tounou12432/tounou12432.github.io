I'm a youth development and digital engagement practitioner from Lao PDR. I design and coordinate programmes that help young people and communities build skills, take part in decisions that affect them, and use technology safely and well.

I studied Community and Rural Development at the National University of Laos, with exchange study at the University of the Ryukyus in Japan and the YSEALI Academic Fellows Program in the United States. Since 2020 my work has grown from youth education and environmental projects in Vientiane to national AI literacy programmes with the ASEAN Foundation and Google.org, and a regional youth project across four countries.

The problems I care about sit where community work meets digital change: making AI literacy practical and responsible, keeping young people safe online, and turning youth voices into input that institutions actually use. I serve on the first Youth Advisory Board to the UN Country Team in Lao PDR, with a focus on digital transformation.

Leading regional teams taught me that good coordination starts with listening: understanding what motivates people and what limits them, then shaping the work around that. Outside programme work I follow internet governance and sustainability, design visual content, and build websites. I speak Lao, Thai, English and some Japanese.
