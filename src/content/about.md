I design and coordinate programmes that help young people and communities in Lao PDR build skills, take part in decisions that affect them, and use technology safely and well.

Since 2020 my work has grown from youth education and environmental projects in Vientiane to national AI literacy programmes with the ASEAN Foundation and Google.org, and a youth project across four countries. I studied Community and Rural Development at the National University of Laos, with exchanges in Japan and the United States.

I care most about practical, responsible AI literacy, online safety for young people, and turning youth voices into input institutions actually use. Good coordination, for me, starts with listening. I speak Lao, Thai, English and some Japanese.
